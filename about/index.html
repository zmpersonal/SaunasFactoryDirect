About us

A clearer way to shop for a home sauna

Saunas Factory Direct organizes scattered sauna listings into an exact-model price index, a quote checker and a supplier directory.

Why this site exists

Home sauna shopping is unusually difficult to compare. The same cabinet can appear under a marketing name, a model number and several dealer listings. Some advertised prices include a heater or roof package; others do not. Freight, electrical work and assembly can also change the final cost by thousands of dollars.

We built Saunas Factory Direct to make the first stage of that comparison faster. The database connects product names to model identifiers, normalizes basic categories, records observed prices and gives each model a permanent research page.

What we cover

The index includes infrared, full-spectrum, traditional, hybrid, indoor, outdoor, barrel, pod and cabin saunas when we can identify a public model and a usable current price. It is intentionally selective rather than exhaustive. A product can be omitted when its model identity, availability or price cannot be verified with reasonable confidence.

How buying links work

Every place-to-buy link first opens a supplier profile on this site. Supplier profiles group the models attributed to that seller and contain one external retailer link. InHouse Wellness receives featured placement and is the only displayed purchase option when it carries the exact model. That rule is explained in our retailer disclosure and methodology.

What we are not

Saunas Factory Direct is not the retailer, manufacturer, installer or warranty provider for the products in the index. We do not take orders or guarantee inventory, delivery dates, specifications or prices. Our role is to help shoppers ask better questions before choosing a seller.
