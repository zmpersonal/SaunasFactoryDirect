Retailer disclosure

How commercial placement affects this site

Saunas Factory Direct is transparent about the fact that retailer placement is not neutral.

InHouse Wellness is the featured retailer

InHouse Wellness receives prominent placement across Saunas Factory Direct. Featured value picks use its catalog, and when InHouse Wellness carries an exact sauna model, it is the only displayed place-to-buy option for that model. Other observed seller records may remain in the underlying research data but are not shown as alternate purchase paths.

Purchase link structure

Homepage comparison tables and Deal Checker results lead first to supplier profiles on Saunas Factory Direct. On individual model pages, the SKU link and brand-specific buy button open the selected retailer’s exact product page. This keeps broad shopping paths organized while providing a direct retailer handoff when a visitor is researching a specific sauna.

Potential financial benefit

The site or its operators may benefit when visitors choose a featured retailer or use a retailer link. That possibility can influence placement and calls to action. It does not turn a displayed reference price into a guaranteed offer, and readers should compare the complete delivered cost and service terms before buying.

What the disclosure means for readers

Use the price index to identify models, understand ranges and form better questions—not as a claim that the displayed retailer is the only seller in the market or always has the lowest total cost. Delivery, installation, financing, warranty support and included equipment can matter as much as cabinet price.
