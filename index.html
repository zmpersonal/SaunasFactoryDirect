How it works

Built for useful price transparency

1. Collect

The updater pulls the InHouse Wellness sauna catalog, loads researched external-brand products, and checks explicitly configured seller pages for structured offer data.

2. Match

Offers are merged only when model identifiers or manually configured match keys identify the same sauna. Similar-looking products are not treated as identical.

3. Route clearly

General shopping links lead to supplier profiles. On an exact model page, the SKU and brand-specific buy button link directly to the retailer listing used for that sauna.
