How it works

Built for useful price transparency

1. Collect

The scheduled updater pulls the InHouse Wellness sauna catalog and can check explicitly configured external product pages for structured offer data.

2. Match

Offers are merged only when model identifiers or manually configured match keys identify the same sauna. Similar-looking products are not treated as identical.

3. Compare

The deal checker compares a user-entered quote with the lowest observed offer and reference price, while showing source and observation date.
