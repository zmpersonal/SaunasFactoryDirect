Methodology

How the sauna price index works

Our goal is simple: make advertised sauna pricing easier to compare without pretending that every listing is equivalent.

Featured catalog source

InHouse Wellness is the featured retailer on this site. Its sauna listings are intentionally shown first when InHouse carries the model. The automated updater attempts to retrieve current product, variant, price and compare-at-price data from the retailer’s public Shopify catalog. If that endpoint becomes unavailable, the updater can fall back to publicly accessible product-page structured data.

External-brand catalog

data/external_products.csv seeds researched sauna models from brands that are not currently represented in the InHouse catalog used by this site. These models can therefore appear in the database even when there is no InHouse offer. Their seller name is shown accurately rather than being labeled as InHouse.

External price sources

data/external_sources.csv contains additional exact-model seller pages for models already in the database. The updater checks those URLs for structured Product/Offer pricing. A manually verified fallback price may be retained in the feed so a temporary bot block does not immediately erase a researched comparison.

Price matching

External offers are merged into a product only when the configured model key or an unambiguous model identifier matches. Model-key generation preserves SKU suffixes such as Elite and FS where available so distinct versions are not collapsed into the same page.

Dealer bundles, optional heaters, upgraded woods, freight, installation and accessories can materially change value and should be checked on the seller page.

Deal ratings

“Excellent,” “Competitive,” “Fair,” and “High” are mechanical comparisons with observed prices, not appraisals of seller quality. A quote at or below the lowest observed active price receives the strongest rating. A slightly higher price may still be worthwhile when shipping, warranty support, dealer authorization, installation or included accessories differ.

MSRP/reference prices

Reference prices usually come from a retailer compare-at price or other public reference price. They are useful context, but a struck-through price does not prove the product commonly sold at that price.

Featured-retailer disclosure

InHouse Wellness receives prominent placement where it carries the model. Lowest-observed calculations include every active valid price source in the local dataset rather than silently forcing the featured retailer to appear cheapest.

Freshness

Automated checks are designed to run weekly and on demand through GitHub Actions. Retailers can change markup, block automated requests, alter structured data, remove a product, or change availability. Always verify the final price on the seller’s site.
